What are your office hours?

Our office is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and UAE public holidays.

How long does visa processing take?

UAE tourist visa processing typically takes 2-3 working days. Express processing is available for urgent cases. Processing time for other countries varies based on the destination.
